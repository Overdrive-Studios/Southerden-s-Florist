About us

Southerden's is one of Toowoomba's oldest and most respected florists.

Welcome to Southerden's Florist, where tradition, passion and family values have been blooming for over 50 years.

Founded by Alwyn and Margaret Southerden in the 1970s, the business began on their Toowoomba flower farm before expanding into retail. What began as a small, family-run business has grown into a trusted name in the community, built on generations of care, creativity and dedication to quality.

For more than five decades our family has taken pride in handcrafting beautiful floral arrangements for every occasion, celebrating life's happiest moments and offering comfort during its most difficult times. Each bouquet reflects not only our experience but the personal touch only a family business can provide.

Today we remain proudly family owned and operated by Lyn and Phil, with multi-generational involvement keeping our local heritage and commitment to design excellence alive. When you choose us, you're not just buying flowers, you're becoming part of our story.
