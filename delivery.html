Delivery

For all your flower deliveries around Toowoomba and the Darling Downs, we go that extra mile. For out-of-town deliveries or places that seem remote, we go where others don't. Just call us.

How it works

2pmOrder cut-off for same-day local delivery, Mon–Fri
2 runsDeliveries each day around Toowoomba, Mon–Fri
FreeNo extra charge for deliveries in Toowoomba
SaturdayBy prior arrangement

We deliver to homes, workplaces, nursing homes and hospitals including Toowoomba Hospital, St Andrew's and St Vincent's.

Good to know

  • Order by 2pm Monday to Friday for same-day local delivery.
  • We make 2 delivery runs a day around Toowoomba, Monday to Friday.
  • Saturday deliveries are by prior arrangement. Please call us.
  • Timed deliveries need to be ordered at least 2 hours before the time you'd like. We'll do our best, but times can't be guaranteed.
  • Delivery within Toowoomba is included in our prices. Some outer areas have a delivery fee, and checkout shows it once you enter the suburb and postcode.
  • Our shop hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 3pm.
