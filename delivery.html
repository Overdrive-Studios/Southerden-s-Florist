Delivery

What does delivery cost?

Delivery is included in the Toowoomba area. Outer areas have a small fee, listed below. Same-day delivery when ordered by 2pm Monday to Friday, unless an earlier time is shown.

Not listed? Give us a call on 07 4638 3622. Orders to other Australian locations are usually $18.

Good to know

  • Order by 2pm Monday to Friday for same-day local delivery.
  • We make 2 delivery runs a day around Toowoomba, Monday to Friday.
  • Saturday deliveries are by prior arrangement. Please call us.
  • Timed deliveries need to be ordered at least 2 hours before the time you'd like. We'll do our best, but times can't be guaranteed.
  • Delivery within Toowoomba is included in our prices. Some outer areas have a delivery fee. See the delivery prices above.
  • Our shop hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 3pm.
