Ordering and delivery

All prices shown are in Australian dollars including GST.

  1. Prices are inclusive, with no extra charges for deliveries in Toowoomba.
  2. We have 2 deliveries each day in the Toowoomba area, Monday to Friday.
  3. Our shop hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 3pm.
  4. All orders must be received by 2pm for same-day local delivery, Monday to Friday, unless otherwise stated in the delivery options.
  5. All timed deliveries must be received at least 2 hours before the specified time, and no times are guaranteed.
  6. Prices include GST (local taxes) where applicable.
  7. The higher price, if selected, indicates an increase in size and/or flower and container value.
  8. All designs illustrated are a guide only.
  9. The style of flowers, gourmet items and gifts delivered may vary with regional and seasonal availability, or when they don't meet our quality standards.
  10. In some cases, substitutions of equivalent value and quality may be necessary to fulfil your order.
